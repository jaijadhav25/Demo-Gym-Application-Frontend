Membership FAQs

How does upgrading work?

When you upgrade mid-cycle, your remaining balance is pro-rated and applied immediately as a discount toward your new tier.

Can I switch between monthly and annual?

Yes, switching to annual saves you up to 20% compared to monthly dues and includes extra perks like free guest passes.

Are there cancellation fees?

No contracts or hidden cancellation fees. You can cancel your renewal anytime with 30 days notice before the next billing cycle.

Does my keycard change?

No, your existing keycard and digital app QR code will automatically unlock upgraded amenities (such as 24/7 branch doors or recovery lounge).
